PURPOSE:
This procedure defines the daily verification of air-displacement pipettes used for analytical sample preparation, so that volume error is detected before it affects a reported result.

SCOPE:
This procedure applies to all Laboratory Analysts and the Laboratory Supervisor using air-displacement pipettes in the analytical laboratory.

DEFINITIONS:
Verification: A gravimetric check of a pipette at one volume against the acceptance limit for that volume.
Acceptance Limit: The maximum permitted mean error and repeatability for a pipette volume, listed in the pipette register.
Pipette Register: The controlled list of pipettes, their volumes, their acceptance limits and their calibration due dates.
Out of Tolerance: A verification result outside the acceptance limit, which removes the pipette from service.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Laboratory Analysts: Perform the verification and record the result
- Laboratory Supervisor: Review out-of-tolerance results and withdraw the pipette
- Metrology Contractor: Perform the annual calibration
- Quality Assurance: Audit the pipette register quarterly

PROCEDURE:

Step 1: Condition the Balance and the Pipette
Switch on the analytical balance and let it stabilise. Draw and expel distilled water three times with the pipette before the first weighing.

Step 2: Record the Environment
Record the water temperature and the room temperature on the verification record. The correction factor for the water temperature is taken from the table in the pipette register.

Step 3: Weigh Ten Deliveries
Deliver ten volumes into the weighing vessel and record each mass. Do not remove the vessel from the balance between deliveries.

Step 4: Calculate Mean Error and Repeatability
Convert each mass to a volume using the correction factor. Calculate the mean error against the nominal volume and the repeatability as the standard deviation of the ten volumes.

Step 5: Compare Against the Acceptance Limit
Compare the mean error and the repeatability against the acceptance limit for that volume in the pipette register. A result outside either limit is out of tolerance.

Step 6: Withdraw an Out-of-Tolerance Pipette
Label an out-of-tolerance pipette DO NOT USE, remove it to the metrology shelf and notify the Laboratory Supervisor the same day.

Step 7: File the Verification Record
Sign the verification record and file it in the pipette register within the same shift.
