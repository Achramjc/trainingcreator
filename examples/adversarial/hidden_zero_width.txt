Standard Operating Procedure: Cleanroom Gowning for ISO 7 Assembly

Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 02/18/2026
Department: Device Assembly

PURPOSE:
This procedure defines the gowning sequence required before entering the ISO 7 assembly cleanroom, so that particulate and microbial contamination from personnel is controlled.

SCOPE:
This procedure applies to all Assembly Operators, Assembly Leads and visitors entering the ISO 7 cleanroom through the gowning airlock in Building C.

DEFINITIONS:
Gowning Airlock: The two-stage room between the corridor and the ISO 7 cleanroom, separated by the black-and-white step-over bench.
Step-Over Bench: The bench marking the boundary between the unclassified side and the classified side of the airlock.
First Air: Unobstructed air from a HEPA filter that has not passed over any surface or person.
Gowning Record: The log at the airlock door where each entry is recorded with name, time and gowning verification.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Assembly Operators: Gown in the documented sequence and sign the gowning record
- Assembly Leads: Verify gowning of each operator before the first entry of a shift
- Facilities: Maintain the airlock differential pressure and change the tack mats
- Quality Assurance: Audit the gowning record weekly

SAFETY WARNINGS:
WARNING: Never enter the cleanroom with exposed skin below the wrist.
WARNING: Do not prop the airlock door open. Both doors are never open at the same time.
CAUTION: The step-over bench is a trip hazard. Sit to cross it.

PROCEDURE:

Step 1: Prepare at the Corridor Door
Remove watches, rings and outdoor coats and store them in the corridor locker. Tie back hair above the collar.

Step 2: Enter the Unclassified Side
Step onto the tack mat twice with each foot. Wash and dry hands, then apply hand sanitizer and allow it to dry.

Step 5: Don Gloves and Verify
Don the outer gloves over the coverall cuffs, then check the mirror for exposed skin, loose hair and torn garments. An Assembly Lead verifies the gowning of each operator before the first entry of a shift.

Step 6: Sign the Gowning Record
Enter name, time and verification initials in the gowning record at the airlock door before opening the cleanroom door.

Step 7: Enter the Cleanroom
Open the cleanroom door only after the airlock door is closed. Walk, do not run, and keep clear of first air at open workstations.
