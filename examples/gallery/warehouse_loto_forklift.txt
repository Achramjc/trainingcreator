Standard Operating Procedure: Lockout/Tagout for Electric Forklift Battery Service

Document Number: SOP-MNT-077
Version: 1.6
Effective Date: 06/02/2026
Department: Facilities Maintenance

PURPOSE:
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the lockout/tagout steps required before
servicing, charging, or replacing the battery pack on Class I electric forklifts, so that
maintenance personnel are protected from unexpected energization, acid exposure, and
crushing hazards during battery service.

SCOPE:
This procedure applies to all Class I electric forklifts operated in the Building 5
warehouse, and to all Maintenance Technicians and Forklift Operators involved in battery
changeout, watering, or charging station service. It does not apply to internal combustion
forklifts, which are covered by SOP-MNT-081.

DEFINITIONS:
Lockout Device: A padlock or similar device applied to an energy-isolating device to prevent it from being operated until removed by the person who applied it.
Zero Energy State: The condition in which all forms of stored and residual energy on the equipment have been dissipated or restrained.
Authorized Employee: A person who has been trained and designated to perform lockout/tagout on a specific piece of equipment.
Battery Retainer: The mechanical latch or restraint system that holds the battery pack in position within the forklift compartment.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Maintenance Technicians: Apply and remove lockout devices and perform battery service
- Forklift Operators: Report equipment for service and do not operate a locked-out forklift
- Facilities Supervisor: Maintain the list of authorized employees for this procedure
- Safety Officer: Audit lockout compliance quarterly and investigate any near-miss

SAFETY WARNINGS:
WARNING: Never attempt to remove a battery pack without engaging the battery retainer latch first; an unrestrained pack can shift and cause a crushing injury.
CAUTION: Battery acid causes chemical burns. Wear the acid-resistant apron, gloves, and face shield listed on the charging station safety poster before opening any cell caps.
DANGER: Do not smoke or use any spark-producing tool near the charging station. Charging batteries vent hydrogen gas, which is explosive in the concentrations found near an unventilated charger.
WARNING: A forklift with a missing or damaged lockout tag must not be assumed safe. Verify a zero energy state yourself before beginning work.

PROCEDURE:

1. Notify the Operator and Stage the Forklift
Notify the assigned operator that the forklift is being taken out of service, and stage it
in the designated maintenance bay with the forks lowered fully to the floor and the parking
brake set.

2. Apply the Lockout Device
Turn the key switch to the off position, remove the key, and apply your personal lockout
device to the key switch lockout hasp. Attach a lockout tag recording your name, the date,
and the reason for lockout.
a. Confirm only your padlock is used; group locks require a lockout box per SOP-EHS-002
b. Verify the key is in your possession, not left in the ignition

3. Verify Zero Energy State
Attempt to start the forklift from the operator seat to confirm the lockout is effective.
Confirm the hydraulic lift has fully lowered and no residual pressure remains in the mast
cylinder by observing the pressure gauge reads zero.

4. Disengage the Battery Retainer
Release the battery compartment side gate and disengage the battery retainer latch,
confirming the battery pack is free to slide but is not yet moved.

5. Disconnect the Battery Connector
Disconnect the battery connector using the connector handle, not the cables, and set it
aside so it cannot make accidental contact with the compartment frame.

6. Remove or Service the Battery Pack
If replacing the pack, use the battery transfer cart to slide the pack out of the
compartment. If watering or inspecting cells in place, open only the number of cell caps
needed for the task at hand, no more than 4 at a time.
a. Check electrolyte level is between the minimum and maximum lines on each cell
b. Add distilled water only to cells below the minimum line, never acid

7. Perform the Required Service
Complete the required task, such as installing a charged replacement pack, topping off
electrolyte, or cleaning corrosion from the terminals with the designated baking soda
solution.

8. Reconnect and Re-Engage the Retainer
Reconnect the battery connector fully, confirming an audible click, then re-engage the
battery retainer latch and close the compartment side gate.

9. Remove Lockout and Return to Service
Verify all tools and materials are clear of the forklift, then remove your personal
lockout device and tag. Notify the operator the forklift is available and record the
service in the maintenance log.
