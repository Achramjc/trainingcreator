Allergen Changeover Procedure for Packaging Line 7

Document Number: SOP-FS-118
Version: 2.4
Effective Date: 03/03/2026
Department: Packaging Operations

## Purpose
This procedure defines the allergen changeover steps required on Packaging Line 7 when
switching from a product containing tree nuts to a product with no declared tree nut
allergen, so that allergen cross-contact is prevented and the changeover is verified
before the next product is released to run, consistent with the plant HACCP plan.

## Scope
This procedure applies to Packaging Line 7 and to all Line Operators, Sanitation
Technicians, and Quality Technicians involved in an allergen changeover. It applies
whenever the scheduled product changes allergen status, and does not apply to same-allergen
product changes, which follow the shorter changeover in SOP-FS-042.

## Definitions
Allergen Cross-Contact: The unintended transfer of an allergen from a food containing it to a food that does not, through shared equipment or airborne dust.
Dedicated Utensil: A cleaning or handling tool marked and stored for use with a single allergen category only.
ATP Swab: A bioluminescence swab test used as an indirect indicator of cleaning effectiveness on food-contact surfaces.
Changeover Verification: The documented check confirming a line is free of the previous allergen before the next product may run.

## Responsibilities
- Line Operators: Perform teardown, dry clean, and reassembly of the packaging line
- Sanitation Technicians: Perform the wet clean and collect ATP swab verification samples
- Quality Technicians: Review swab results and sign the changeover verification record
- Shift Supervisor: Authorize the line to start the next product after verification passes

## Safety Warnings
WARNING: Do not begin teardown until the line has been locked out per SOP-EHS-002 and the conveyor drive has come to a complete stop.
CAUTION: Sanitizer used in the wet clean step is corrosive at use concentration. Wear the face shield and chemical-resistant gloves listed on the safety data sheet.
WARNING: Any tool, apron, or glove used during the tree nut product run must not be reused during changeover unless it has passed the wet clean and swab verification steps.

## Procedure

### Step 1: Stop the Line and Remove Product
Stop the line at the end of the current production run and remove all remaining tree nut
product, work-in-process, and open packaging materials from the line to a segregated
allergen holding area.
a. Confirm the production count on the batch record before removal
b. Tag all removed material with the allergen holding label

### Step 2: Perform Dry Cleanup
Remove gross product residue and packaging debris from all line surfaces using
dedicated tree nut cleaning tools. Vacuum accessible surfaces rather than using compressed
air, which can aerosolize allergen dust into the surrounding area.

### Step 3: Disassemble Changeover Parts
Disassemble the changeguide rails, filling head nozzles, and the 12 product contact
guides identified on the changeover parts list, and stage them for the wet clean station.
a. Confirm each removed part is tagged with its line location
b. Confirm no part is left on the line frame after disassembly

### Step 4: Perform the Wet Clean
Wash all disassembled parts and fixed line surfaces with the approved sanitizer at 165
degrees Fahrenheit for a minimum contact time of 10 minutes, using dedicated allergen
changeover brushes only.

### Step 5: Rinse and Air Dry
Rinse all cleaned surfaces and parts with potable water and allow to air dry on the
designated clean rack. Do not towel-dry, as reused towels are a documented cross-contact
route on this line.

### Step 6: Collect ATP Swab Samples
Collect ATP swab samples from the eight designated verification points, including the
filling head nozzles and the changeguide rails. A reading below 10 relative light units
at each point passes; a reading at or above 10 fails and requires re-cleaning.

### Step 7: Visually Inspect Before Reassembly
Perform a visual inspection of all surfaces under line lighting for any visible product
residue. Any surface with visible residue fails inspection regardless of its swab result
and must be re-cleaned before reassembly.

### Step 8: Reassemble the Line
Reassemble the changeguide rails, filling head nozzles, and product contact guides in
their tagged locations, confirming each of the 12 parts is accounted for against the
changeover parts list.

### Step 9: Complete the Changeover Verification Record
Record the ATP swab results, visual inspection outcome, and reassembly confirmation on
the Changeover Verification Record. The Quality Technician reviews the record and signs
before the next product may be introduced to the line.

### Step 10: Authorize Line Startup
The Shift Supervisor reviews the signed Changeover Verification Record and authorizes
line startup for the next product, recording the start time and the new product's lot
number on the production schedule.
