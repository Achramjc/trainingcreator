Standard Operating Procedure: Equipment Cleaning and Swab Sampling Between Product Changeovers

Document Number: SOP-MFG-221
Version: 4.0
Effective Date: 01/20/2026
Department: Manufacturing - Solid Dose

1.0 PURPOSE
This procedure defines the cleaning, swab sampling, and residue-limit verification
required on the V-blender and tablet press train before a different drug product is
introduced to the equipment train, so that cross-contamination between products is
prevented and the equipment is released for the next changeover with objective evidence.

2.0 SCOPE
This procedure applies to all equipment in Suite 2 shared between more than one drug
product, and to all Manufacturing Operators, Cleaning Verification Technicians, and
Quality Assurance staff involved in changeover cleaning. It does not apply to dedicated
single-product equipment, which follows SOP-MFG-104.

3.0 DEFINITIONS
Maximum Allowable Carryover (MACO): The calculated maximum quantity of the previous product's active ingredient that may remain on shared equipment surfaces without posing a safety risk to the next product.
Swab Sample: A defined 25 square centimeter surface area sampled with a wetted swab and submitted for residue analysis.
Clean Hold Time: The maximum 72-hour interval during which cleaned equipment may sit idle before requiring re-cleaning prior to use.
Rinse Sample: A sample of the final rinse water collected to verify residue and detergent removal on surfaces a swab cannot reach.

4.0 RESPONSIBILITIES
- Manufacturing Operators: Perform the cleaning cycle and complete the cleaning log
- Cleaning Verification Technicians: Collect swab and rinse samples per this procedure
- Quality Assurance: Review results against the MACO limit and release equipment for use
- Laboratory: Analyze swab and rinse samples and report results within 48 hours

SAFETY WARNINGS
WARNING: Do not enter the blender bowl until lockout/tagout has been applied to the drive
motor and verified with a zero-energy check.
CAUTION: Cleaning solvent is flammable. No open flame or spark-producing tools are
permitted within 10 feet of the cleaning station while solvent is in use.
WARNING: Respiratory protection rated for the previous product's occupational exposure
band must be worn until swab results confirm the surface is below the MACO limit.
DANGER: Never bypass the tablet press guard interlock to reach cleaning solvent into the
compression zone while the turret is capable of rotating.

5.0 PROCEDURE
5.1 Verify Lockout and Disassembly
Apply lockout/tagout to the equipment train per SOP-EHS-002 and verify zero energy state.
Disassemble removable product-contact parts, including the blender bowl liner, press
tooling, and dust collection ductwork, per the equipment-specific teardown instruction.

5.2 Perform the Detergent Wash Cycle
Wash all product-contact surfaces with the qualified alkaline detergent at 55 degrees
Celsius for a minimum of 15 minutes of contact time, using a soft nylon brush on
hard-to-reach surfaces such as the blender shaft seal.
a. Confirm the detergent concentration is between 1.0 and 1.5 percent by volume
b. Confirm wash water temperature with the calibrated thermometer before starting the timer

5.3 Perform the Final Rinse
Rinse all surfaces with purified water until the conductivity of the rinse effluent is at
or below 5 microsiemens per centimeter, confirmed with the in-line conductivity meter.
Collect a rinse sample from the final rinse of the blender bowl for laboratory analysis.

5.4 Visually Inspect All Surfaces
Inspect all product-contact surfaces under white light of at least 500 lux for visible
residue, discoloration, or detergent film. Any surface showing visible residue fails
inspection and must be re-cleaned before swabbing.

5.5 Collect Swab Samples
Collect swab samples from the six designated locations shown on the equipment cleaning
map, including the blender shaft seal, the discharge valve, and the press die table. Each
swab covers a 25 square centimeter area using the validated swabbing technique.
a. Use a new sterile swab and glove pair for each location
b. Label each swab vial with the location code, lot number, and date

5.6 Submit Samples and Apply the Clean Hold Label
Submit the swab and rinse samples to the laboratory with a completed chain-of-custody
form. Apply the green Clean Hold label to the equipment, recording the cleaning
completion time; the 72-hour clean hold clock begins at this timestamp.

5.7 Review Laboratory Results Against the MACO Limit
Quality Assurance reviews the reported residue result for each swab location against the
calculated MACO limit of 4.0 micrograms per 25 square centimeters. Any result exceeding
the MACO limit fails the cleaning verification and equipment must be re-cleaned and
re-sampled.

5.8 Release the Equipment for the Next Product
Once all six swab results and the rinse sample pass, Quality Assurance signs the
Equipment Release Log, records the next product and lot number authorized to use the
equipment, and removes the Clean Hold label, replacing it with the equipment status tag.

5.9 Reassemble and Verify Clean Hold Time at Use
Reassemble the equipment using only the parts identified on the teardown record. Before
production start, confirm the elapsed time since the cleaning completion timestamp does
not exceed the 72-hour clean hold limit; if exceeded, the equipment must be re-cleaned.
