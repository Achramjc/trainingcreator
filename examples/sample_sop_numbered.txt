Standard Operating Procedure: Widget Calibration

Version: 1.3
Effective Date: 03/05/2026

1.0 PURPOSE
This procedure defines the steps required to calibrate the widget press prior to each production run, ensuring dimensional accuracy and operator safety.

2.0 SCOPE
This procedure applies to all calibration technicians operating widget presses in Building B.

3.0 DEFINITIONS
Calibration Target (CT): The reference measurement used to validate press alignment.
Press Operator: The technician assigned to run the calibration sequence.

RESPONSIBILITIES
* Calibration Technicians: Perform the calibration sequence and record results
* Shift Supervisors: Verify calibration records prior to production start

SAFETY WARNINGS
WARNING: Disconnect power before adjusting the press alignment bolts.
CAUTION: Calibration targets are sharp; handle with gloves.

4.0 PROCEDURE
4.1 Power Down the Press
Switch off the main power breaker and confirm zero energy state before beginning calibration.

4.2 Attach Calibration Target
Mount the calibration target to the press head using the two mounting screws.
a. Align the target with the reference notch
b. Tighten both mounting screws evenly

4.3 Record Baseline Reading
Read the digital gauge and record the baseline value on Form WC-201.

REFERENCES
- Form WC-201: Calibration Record
